Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

SSO ID Portal respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information on this website.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information such as your name, mobile number, email, Aadhaar, SSO ID, password, or OTP.

This website does not have any login forms or registration forms.

2. Cookies & Analytics

We may use basic analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) to understand website traffic. These tools collect anonymous data like page views and device type. No personal identification is done.

3. Third-Party Links

This website contains links to official government websites (like sso.rajasthan.gov.in). When you click on those links, you leave our website. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites.

4. Data Security

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no personal information stored on our servers.

5. Children’s Privacy

This website is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach us through the contact information available on the website (if provided).
