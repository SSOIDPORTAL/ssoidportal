How to Fix Common Rajasthan SSO Login Problems

Being unable to sign in can be stressful, especially when you need a government service or an application deadline is near. Work through the checks below one at a time. This website is an independent guide; it cannot access, unlock or recover an account for you.

Safety first: Open the official Rajasthan SSO website directly: sso.rajasthan.gov.in/signin. Never share your password, OTP or recovery details with another person or enter them on this information website.

1. The page will not load

  1. Check whether other websites open normally.
  2. Refresh the official page and wait briefly before retrying.
  3. Try an updated browser or a private/incognito window.
  4. If the problem continues on different devices or networks, it may be a temporary service issue. Try again later and use official support channels.

2. OTP is not arriving

  • Confirm that your phone has mobile network coverage and can receive messages.
  • Check blocked messages and SMS filtering.
  • Use the resend option only after the countdown permits it; repeated rapid requests may not help.
  • Make sure you are using the mobile number or account method associated with your existing profile.
  • Do not tell anyone an OTP, even if they claim they can fix the account.

3. Password is rejected

  1. Check Caps Lock and make sure there are no accidental spaces.
  2. Use the official portal's password recovery option rather than repeatedly guessing.
  3. Follow only the instructions displayed on the official website. Recovery options can change.
  4. After regaining access, create a unique password that you do not use on other sites.

4. You see “account already exists”

Do not keep creating new accounts with different details. First try the official SSO ID recovery process. If your identity or registered contact information no longer matches, ask the official help channel or an authorised e-Mitra centre what documents and process are currently accepted.

5. Captcha or browser errors

  • Enable JavaScript and cookies for the official website.
  • Temporarily disable aggressive content-blocking extensions for that official page only.
  • Clear the site's cached files, then reload it.
  • Try another current browser. Avoid installing unknown “SSO fixer” apps or extensions.

6. You need help with an application deadline

Keep a note of the error text, date and approximate time. Take a screenshot that does not expose passwords, OTPs, Aadhaar details or other sensitive information. Check the relevant recruitment or service portal for official notices and helpdesk instructions. Do not rely on social media posts alone for deadlines.
