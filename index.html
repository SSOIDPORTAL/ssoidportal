What is Rajasthan SSO ID?

SSO ID (Single Sign-On ID) is a unique digital identity issued by the Government of Rajasthan.

With one SSO ID and password, you can access more than 100 government services including recruitment, scholarships, e-Mitra, Jan Aadhaar, and employee services.

How to Create SSO ID (Registration Process)

Registration is completely free and takes only a few minutes.

Step-by-Step Process

  1. Visit the official registration page: sso.rajasthan.gov.in/register
  2. Select your user type:
    • Citizen – for residents and students
    • Udyog – for business owners
    • Govt. Employee – for state government staff
  3. Choose registration method:
    • Citizen → Jan Aadhaar / Google / Aadhaar
    • Employee → SIPF Number
    • Udyog → BRN or Udyog Aadhaar
  4. Verify the OTP sent to your registered mobile number
  5. Create your SSO ID (Username) and set a strong password
  6. Complete the process. Your SSO ID will be sent via SMS and email

Note: Your SSO ID (Username) cannot be changed later. Choose carefully.

One-Time Registration (OTR) 2026

OTR is required for most government job applications in Rajasthan.

Once you complete OTR, you can use the same profile for multiple recruitment applications.

  1. Login with your SSO ID
  2. Open the Recruitment Portal

Frequently Asked Questions

Where should I create my SSO ID?

You should create your SSO ID only on the official website: sso.rajasthan.gov.in/register

How long does registration take?

Usually 5 to 10 minutes if all details are ready.

Can one person have multiple SSO IDs?

Yes, but it is better to merge them into one account.

What if my registered mobile number has changed?

Visit the nearest e-Mitra centre with Aadhaar or Jan Aadhaar to update it.

Is registration free?

Yes, creating an SSO ID is completely free.

Can I use the same SSO ID for scholarships and jobs?

Yes. One SSO ID works for recruitment, scholarships, e-Mitra and many other services.

How can I recover my forgotten SSO ID?

Send RJ SSO to 9223166166 from your registered mobile number, or use the official recovery page.

How can I reset my password?

Send RJ SSO PASSWORD to 9223166166, or use the “Forgot Password” option on the official login page.
