Frequently Asked Questions

Is SSO ID Portal the official Rajasthan SSO website?

No. This website is an independent guide. The official SSO service is hosted on the Rajasthan government domain sso.rajasthan.gov.in.

Does this website collect my SSO password or OTP?

No login form is provided here. Official actions open in a new tab on the government website.

Where should I create an SSO ID?

Use the official registration page linked on this website. Check the address bar before entering personal information.

What if I forgot my SSO ID or password?

Open the official SSO sign-in page and use its account-recovery links.

Can I use this website to submit a government application?

No. This site provides information and navigation only. Actual submissions should be completed on the relevant official government portal.
