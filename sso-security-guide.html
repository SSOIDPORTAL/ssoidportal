How to Keep Your Rajasthan SSO Account Safe

A single sign-on account can connect you to several services, so protecting access matters. The most useful habit is simple: enter account details only on the official website you intended to visit, and treat unexpected requests for passwords or OTPs as suspicious.

Remember: This website is not the Rajasthan government portal and will never need your SSO password, OTP, Aadhaar number or recovery code.

Use the official domain

Start from the official SSO sign-in page. Check the address bar carefully. Search advertisements, forwarded messages and look-alike spellings can lead to unrelated websites. A padlock alone does not prove a site is genuine.

Create a strong, unique password

  • Use a long password that is difficult to guess.
  • Do not reuse the same password for email, social media and government services.
  • Avoid names, birthdays, phone numbers and common sequences.
  • Do not store passwords in public or shared computers.

Keep OTPs private

An OTP is a temporary security code, not a customer-support number. Do not read it aloud to a caller or send it in a chat. A person who knows your OTP may be able to approve an action on your account.

Recognise common scams

  • A caller says your account will be blocked unless you share an OTP immediately.
  • A message promises a government benefit but asks for a fee or login credentials on an unfamiliar site.
  • A social-media account claims it can recover your ID if you send identity documents or pay privately.
  • A website copies official colours and logos but uses a different web address.

Pause, do not click the message link, and independently open the official portal. Verify schemes, application dates and fees through official notices.

Using a shared device

  1. Do not select “remember me” on a public computer.
  2. Sign out when finished.
  3. Close the browser window and avoid downloading identity documents to a shared device.
  4. If you suspect that you left a session open, use the official account-security or password-recovery options.

If you suspect unauthorised access

Use the official portal to secure the account as soon as possible. If the same password was used elsewhere, change it on those services too. Keep evidence of suspicious messages, but do not post personal details publicly. Use official government or cybercrime reporting channels for guidance appropriate to your situation.

Official starting point

Rajasthan SSO official sign-in. This independent guide is educational and does not provide account recovery or government services. Procedures can change, so check official instructions.
