About SSO ID Portal

SSO ID Portal is an independent informational website created to help people easily find the official links and step-by-step guidance related to Rajasthan SSO ID (Single Sign-On).

Our Purpose

Many people face difficulty in finding the correct official website for SSO Login, Registration, Forgot Password, and OTR. Our goal is to provide clear, simple, and accurate information along with direct links to the official government portals.

What We Provide

  • Direct links to official SSO Login and Registration pages
  • Step-by-step guides for creating SSO ID
  • Help for Forgot SSO ID and Password recovery
  • Information about One-Time Registration (OTR)
  • Common problems and their solutions

Important Note

This website is not affiliated with the Government of Rajasthan or any government department. We do not collect any personal information, passwords, or OTPs. All important actions are redirected to the official website sso.rajasthan.gov.in.

We try our best to keep the information updated and accurate. However, government processes and links may change. Always verify details on the official portal before taking any action.
